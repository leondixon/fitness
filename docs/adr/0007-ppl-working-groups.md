# Activation map is a prerequisite; PPL is only a Pick label

Do not generate real Picks until Exercises and yoga types have Activation on Muscles — at least an approximation. Push/pull/legs labels the lifting Pick; it is not the fatigue, overlap, or transfer engine. The map may be filled by an upfront research pass and/or lazily when an Exercise is first chosen as suitable, via deterministic model rating of listed Exercises. Shipping PPL-as-proxy was rejected because it cannot express "90% rhomboids" or cross-group overlap.
