# Fatigue follows Activation; same-group strength does not stack day-to-day

Fatigue follows overlapping Muscle Activation, not Quality and not PPL labels. Strength as a Quality may be Picked on consecutive days (push strength then pull strength is fine). The same Working group at strength may not (push strength then push strength the next day is not). Yoga Intensity comes from type via Activation, not a uniform full-body veto. If a Quality stays behind, Check-in may Hold runs or yoga so that Quality can land. A day-count "too fatigued" rule is rejected.
