# General fitness via Qualities

The Goal is general fitness, not one primary adaptation. Check-in keeps four Qualities from starving: strength, hypertrophy, conditioning, and mobility. Fat loss and injury-avoidance are not Qualities; Injuries stay Constraints. Composition may Flag overeating (ADR-0012) without becoming a fifth Quality. The alternative was a single primary (e.g. strength) with yoga and running as mere Activity; that was rejected because the athlete wants to stay well-rounded.
